About UPMA's MyLu

Quick urban
commute, elevated.

An eVTOL prototype with AUW of 200 Kg, designed to carry a single pilot over 10–12 km in a quarter of an hour — and grow into 40 km, 1-hour endurance machines.

The medium-term vision

Pilot MyLu manually with minimal skill — as long as take-off and landing spots are flat with a 10 m × 10 m clear area. Endurance of at least 1 hour and a 40 km range with all applicable compliance.

Enable FSP — Fully Self Piloted System based on fusion of LiDAR and Computer Vision, so flying becomes as simple as pressing a destination.

Look for better ways to commute through effective utilization of air medium and provide newer means of value chain creation.
